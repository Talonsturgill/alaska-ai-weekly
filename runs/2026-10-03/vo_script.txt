The case against trusting a machine with the news?
Walter makes no calls, attends no meetings, and interviews nobody. Reporting still takes people.
Some stories go up with no human first. Alaska News's editor checks afterward.
Herz still says reporters who discount AI's value are burying their heads in the sand.
Writing isn't the hard part anymore. Choosing the top story is.
The Press reports Alaska News's founders include a conservative pollster and a spokesperson for the governor.
Walter's own answer? Those backgrounds create a real trust burden.
Whoever picks the top story picks what Alaskans read first.
The writing's cheap now. The choosing isn't.
