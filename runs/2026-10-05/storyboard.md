# Every Door storyboard 2026-10-05

At UAA the AI rule hangs on each classroom door behind one handbook sentence that never says AI, so the film makes the rule a row of doors that all carry the SAME coral AI glyph with a different navy verdict mark at every one, and asks whether November's guiding principles should add one shared default.

## Angle
At UAA the AI rule lives in each instructor's syllabus, behind one handbook sentence about devices that never mentions AI, so a count of 119 AI cases of 150 measures how often the rule was invoked more than how much students use AI, and November's guiding principles can at best give every syllabus a shared starting point.
