NuCube builds a small nuclear reactor called NuSun, so how big is it, where does it go, how much, and when?
The deal papers skip all that, but they do state the share price. AIDEA agreed to pay nine point two five dollars a share, about fourteen point five percent below the reference price of ten eighty two, plus half a warrant to buy more at eleven fifty.
NuCube is merging with an acquisition company called Launch Two, and the merger needs seventy five million dollars in cash to close. AIDEA's money counts toward it.
